# Online Appendix

Sections B.1 to B.5 (Kythera cohort construction, Kaplan-Meier entry estimates,
SMM moments, the instrumental-variables abandonment estimate, and the
Blume-Kohout and Sood placebo) depend on the restricted Medicare closed-claims
extract and are supplied by the author from the restricted-data scripts under
the data use agreement; they are not reproducible from this repository.

Section B.6 is fully reproducible here:

- `output/iso_savings_frontier.csv`: for each small-molecule clock on a grid,
  the biologic clock that holds savings at the current-law level and the
  resulting forgone drugs and net welfare.
- `output/bootstrap_net_welfare.csv`: the 2,000 bootstrap draws of net welfare
  for every policy in Table III.
- `output/table_sensitivity.csv`: the full sensitivity grid behind Table V.
